Gloamhollow

    
Under the hill lies Ilvaris, the Candle-Eater, sealed in with every light she ever stole. Her tomb was built to kill thieves. Most of its traps kill outright, so read everything, test the floor with your pole and pebbles, and think about your lantern. When you die, you rise again at the last chamber you reached, and the traps you have found stay found.
